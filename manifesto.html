Campaign Manifesto

Organizing a stronger, more united, and more responsive KUSU.

This manifesto sets out the direction Benjamin Ruto proposes as National Organising Secretary.

1. Stronger National–Branch Coordination

Build a better working relationship between National Office and branches so programs, responses, and mobilisation are timely and organized.

2. More Efficient Union Operations

Promote structure, planning, and operational discipline so the Union works with consistency, clarity, and speed.

3. Inclusive Delegate and Member Engagement

Strengthen consultation and communication with delegates and members to make leadership more accessible and participatory.

4. Action-Driven Service

Move beyond rhetoric and focus on implementation, coordination, and follow-through in all organizing functions of the Union.

5. One Union, One Vision

Promote unity across branches while respecting local realities, so every member feels represented and connected to the national agenda.

The Appeal

Delegates, the decision is yours.

Let us choose leadership that understands the ground. Let us choose experience that delivers. Let us choose unity that builds.
